Setting up

  1. Print the keyboard at 100% size (“Actual size”, not “Fit to page”) on A4 landscape. Cut along the dashed lines, overlap the sheets on the grey strips, tape them together and tape the strip to the desk. Keep the black square markers uncovered.
  2. Place the camera on the far side of the paper, facing you and tilted down at your hands (about 45°) — a phone on a stand or a pile of books works well. On a phone the app uses the front camera so you can see the screen.
  3. Press Start and allow the camera and microphone. The set-up screen ticks each step off as it's done: paper markers found, microphone hearing taps, both hands seen.
  4. Headphones are best. The microphone listens for your taps, and piano sound from a speaker can confuse it (there's a Speakers setting if you must).

No printer? Press Desk in the top bar and tap middle C, then the C above it, on the bare desk. An optional third tap (two octaves above middle C) makes the far ends more accurate. Press R or Recentre to re-tap middle C if your hands drift.

Playing

  • Tap the keys with the pad of your finger, firmly enough to make a small “tok” — the microphone decides when a note plays and how loud; the camera decides which key.
  • A note lasts while your finger stays down and stops when you lift it. Hovering or resting hands don't play.
  • Sustain pedal: the Pedal button, or hold Space. Better: a cheap USB or Bluetooth foot pedal (“page turner” pedals that send Page Up/Down or arrow keys) works as the sustain pedal, and so does a MIDI sustain pedal on a MIDI keyboard or adapter (Settings → MIDI → Find devices). Settings → “Pedal always on”.
  • Loud and soft: tap harder or softer. After a few notes the app learns your own softest and hardest taps. Settings → Touch makes it lighter or heavier.

Getting it right

  • Teach my fingers (set-up screen, 30 seconds): rest a hand on the keys and tap three times with each finger. The app learns how your fingers move together, which helps it pick the finger that actually pressed.
  • Check accuracy (set-up screen): play C D E F G F E D C. You get a score — and if notes were missed or wrong, the app replays what you played with slightly different detection settings and offers to keep the ones that suit you. Forget undoes taught fingers and tuning.
  • Watch the orange dots on the on-screen keyboard: one per fingertip the camera sees. If they're on the wrong keys, move the camera so the whole paper is in view.
  • The Mic bar flashes green when a tap became a note and orange when a tap was heard but no pressing finger was seen.

The microphone matters most. If your webcam is high up, pick the laptop's or phone's own microphone in Settings → Devices, as close to the paper as possible. Keep your other fingers still while one presses.

Settings → Response: “More accurate” waits a moment longer for the camera; “Faster” plays sooner but picks the wrong finger more often. Chords within one hand are off by default (they can add extra notes).

What's on screen

  • The on-screen keyboard lights up the notes you play (blue), with fingertip dots (orange). You can also play it by touch or with the computer keys A–; (black keys W E T Y U O P, Z/X octave).
  • The camera picture shows the paper outline, your hand points and the keys being played.
  • Settings → Debug view (or add ?debug=1 to the address): frame rate, measured delay from tap to sound, the reason a tap didn't play, and “Copy diagnostics” (numbers only).

Extras

  • Sounds: grand, bright and honky-tonk piano, electric piano, organ, harpsichord, strings, vibraphone, music box, bass — layer two together, or split the keyboard below middle C. Room reverb.
  • Follow a tune: pick a tune and the next key lights up green; wrong notes flash red.
  • ● Rec records what you play; play it back or save it as a MIDI file. Settings → MIDI output sends notes to a real keyboard or music app.
  • ♩ metronome (tempo in Settings), ⛶ full screen.
  • Works offline after your first visit.

Privacy

The camera and microphone are analysed on your device and never recorded, uploaded or saved. Only your settings are kept, in this browser.
