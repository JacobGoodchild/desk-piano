Printable paper keyboard ← back to the piano
Print at 100% / “Actual size” (turn off “Fit to page”), A4 landscape. Check the 10 cm ruler on sheet 1. Cut each sheet along its dashed ✂ cut line on the right, lay it over the grey overlap strip of the next sheet so the key lines continue, and tape it. Tape the strip to the desk. Keep the black square markers uncovered and visible to the camera. No printer? The app also has a Desk mode that works on a plain desk.
