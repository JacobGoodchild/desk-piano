Desk Piano

Print keyboard Press Start. Camera and microphone stay on this device – nothing is recorded or uploaded.
Camera off

Set-up

  1. Print the paper keyboard at 100% and tape it to the desk.
  2. Put the phone (front camera, screen facing you, landscape) or webcam on the far side of the paper, facing you, tilted down about 45° — a stand or a pile of books.
  3. Move it until the whole paper is inside the frame and every marker is ticked (missing ones are outlined red).
  4. Rest both hands on the keys: both should be found. Then tap a few notes.
  5. No paper? Choose “Plain desk” in Settings, then tap middle C and the C above to calibrate.
🎧 Headphones recommended – the microphone listens for your taps, so the piano sound from speakers can confuse it.

      

      
Computer keys: A–; play C4–E5 (W E T Y U O P = black keys), Z/X octave, Shift = loud, Ctrl = soft, Space = pedal, R = recentre (plain desk).
